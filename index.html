About Us

ExamYog was built by a group of friends with one shared goal — to bring simplicity, clarity, and structure to the competitive exam preparation process.

Through our platforms including WBCS.live, TuitionBondhu.com, and WBCSMockTest.live, we focus on solving real problems faced by aspirants at every stage of their preparation journey.

We build practical tools, learning resources, and support systems designed to remove confusion and help students prepare smarter.

Our Platforms

WBCS.live

Focused on competitive exams conducted across West Bengal. We provide preparation resources for WBCS Prelims & Mains, KP SI & Constable, ANM & GNM, WBPSC Miscellaneous, Food SI, Clerkship, Group C & D, and other regional examinations.

WBCSMockTest.live

A dedicated practice and current affairs platform featuring regular blogs on important hot topics, essential static GK content, and a free test section with daily practice sets across multiple subjects.

TuitionBondhu.com

Built for WBBSE and WBCHSE students, Tuition Bondhu offers structured single-topic video tutorials across subjects, helping students learn with clarity and focus. It also serves as a search engine for math problems and academic topics from books covering Classes 5 through 12.
